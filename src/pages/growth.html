The issue isn't motivation. It's that nobody showed them how to turn AI into a repeatable system for their own work. That's what these four weeks are for.

The four-week journey

One week, one step forward.

A shared foundation, with every session applied to your own studies, job or business.

Week 1
Understand & select AI

AI foundations and choosing the right tools

How LLMs work, why outputs vary, hallucinations and context. Compare tools on your actual tasks, then learn a prompting framework (task, context, constraints, examples, format) and improve results through iteration.

You get: personal AI toolkit + reusable prompt framework
Week 2
Use AI in real work

From experimenting to consistent results

Break tasks down, choose the right level of AI help, then build and test your own workflow. We validate the results and find where it fails.

You get: one documented workflow you can reuse on your own
Week 3
Build repeatable workflows

Advanced workflows, integrations & automation

When to use a saved prompt, a template, a regular automation or an AI agent. Build a multi-step workflow or small automation, then test edge cases, privacy and failure handling.

You get: a multi-step workflow or automation blueprint
Week 4
Complete a project

Capstone project & working on your own

Define success criteria, plan and build your project together, test it, then explain or reproduce the key steps yourself to prove you can.

You get: completed mini-project, docs, AI reference sheet, 30-day plan

Between sessions: a short practical assignment, e.g. use your workflow on three real tasks and note results, errors and time taken.
