Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: {{cfg:policies.lastUpdated}}

In short

All payments are final and non-refundable, with one exception: if I fail to deliver a session you have paid for, you get a refund for that session.

This policy applies to all programs offered by {{cfg:person.name}} under the name {{cfg:site.name}}: the AI Clarity Session, the AI Growth Plan and the AI Implementation Intensive.

1. Payments are non-refundable

Each program reserves dedicated one-to-one time that can't be offered to anyone else. For this reason, payments are not refunded for a change of mind, a change in schedule or availability, missed sessions, or dissatisfaction with outcomes.

2. The exception: sessions I fail to deliver

You're entitled to a refund for any paid session that I fail to deliver. A session counts as not delivered when:

  • I cancel or don't attend a scheduled session, and I can't offer you a replacement slot within {{cfg:policies.refundRescheduleWindowDays}} days, or you reasonably decline the replacement offered; or
  • A technical problem on my side prevents the session from taking place and it isn't made up within {{cfg:policies.refundRescheduleWindowDays}} days.

3. How much is refunded

  • AI Clarity Session: the full amount paid.
  • AI Growth Plan: the amount paid divided by four, for each undelivered session.
  • AI Implementation Intensive: the amount paid divided by four, for each undelivered session, unless your written scope says otherwise.

Third-party costs you've paid directly (software subscriptions, API usage, hosting) are not covered, because they're paid to those providers.

4. What isn't eligible for a refund

  • Sessions you miss or cancel with less than {{cfg:policies.rescheduleNoticeHours}} hours' notice. These count as delivered.
  • Growth Plan sessions not booked within the {{cfg:policies.growthValidityWeeks}}-week validity period, unless an extension was agreed.
  • Results such as income, employment, grades or business growth. Training covers the agreed process and deliverables, not outcomes.
  • A change of mind after payment.

5. Rescheduling

You can reschedule each session {{cfg:policies.reschedulesPerSession}} time with at least {{cfg:policies.rescheduleNoticeHours}} hours' notice by WhatsApp or email. If I need to reschedule, I'll tell you as early as possible and offer new slots.

6. How to request a refund

Message me on WhatsApp or email with your name, program and payment ID. Eligible refunds are processed within {{cfg:policies.refundProcessingDays}} working days to your original payment method via Razorpay. Your bank may take a further 5–7 working days to show the credit.
