# **Roadmap 1: AI Clarity Session**

## **Product definition**

**Promise**  
In one personalized session, understand how to use AI for a specific personal, academic, professional, or business goal.

This is your entry product. It should feel complete in itself, not like a deliberately incomplete sample of a larger course.

**Ideal customer**

Someone who is overwhelmed by AI tools, has a clear problem to solve, or wants expert guidance without committing to a four-week program.

**Format**

75–90 minutes live, online or in person, preceded by a short needs assessment.

## **Detailed session plan**

| Time | Segment | What you teach and do |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 0–10 min | Discovery | Identify their goal, current skills, tools, and biggest obstacle. |
| 10–25 min | AI fundamentals | Explain relevant AI concepts, capabilities, limitations, privacy, and verification. |
| 25–45 min | Personalized tool selection | Choose appropriate tools for the customer's use case and demonstrate why they fit. |
| 45–70 min | Guided application | Solve one real problem together using a structured workflow. |
| 70–80 min | Independent attempt | Have the customer reproduce or adapt the workflow themselves. |
| 80–90 min | Action plan | Summarize the workflow, mistakes to avoid, and next steps. |

The precise agenda can shift depending on whether the customer wants to learn, research, code, create content, or improve business productivity.

### **Personalization paths**

* Students: Research, studying, revision, presentations, project planning, and responsible AI use.  
* Professionals: Document analysis, writing, research, meeting preparation, and productivity.  
* Founders and business owners: AI opportunities, workflow bottlenecks, customer communication, and process automation.  
* Technical learners: AI-assisted coding, debugging, APIs, model selection, and prototyping.

### **Deliverables**

* A personalized AI toolkit recommendation.  
* A reusable workflow or prompt template.  
* A short written action plan.  
* A clear recommendation on whether further training is necessary.

**Objective:** Develop a mental model for AI and learn how to select the right tool for the right task.

| Time | Session activity |
| ----- | ----- |

## **6\. Commercial rules I'd establish before launch**

* **Payment:** Collect full payment before a single session. For four-week and implementation packages, prefer upfront payment; if affordability is a concern, use a clearly defined instalment schedule.  
* **Rescheduling:** Allow one reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice. Publish the policy before payment.  
* **Validity:** Complete four-week programs within six weeks unless an exception is agreed.  
* **Support:** Define exactly what follow-up is included. No unlimited WhatsApp consulting.  
* **Software and API costs:** Customer pays third-party costs where applicable, with approval before incurring them.  
* **Implementation scope:** Specify the exact deliverable, acceptance criteria, exclusions, and ownership terms in writing.  
* **Results:** Promise the training process and agreed deliverables, not guaranteed income, employment, business growth, or flawless AI output.

## **7\. Your launch plan**

For the first month, keep the operation lean.

1. Publish the AI Growth program as your flagship offer, with the single session as an entry point.  
2. Keep the Implementation Intensive available for qualified prospects, but don't spend heavily advertising it until you have defined project examples and proof.  
3. Run the first few programs personally and record the most common questions, assignments, and customer outcomes.  
4. Collect honest testimonials and create case studies with permission. Show what the customer learned or built, not just that they enjoyed the session.  
5. Review conversion rates, delivery time, customer feedback, and profitability before increasing your prices or adding more offers.
